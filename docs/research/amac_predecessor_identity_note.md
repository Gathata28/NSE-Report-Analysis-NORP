# AMAC PLC — Predecessor-Name Evidence

**Source:** [AMAC PLC official website](https://amacplc.com/)

**Retrieved:** 27 August 2026

The official AMAC website describes the issuer as **“AMAC PLC (Africa Mega Corp PLC)”** and states that it was **“Formerly Kenya Orchards Ltd”** and rebranded in 2024. This supports a cautious cross-identity mapping of selected Kenya Orchards historical reports to the current AMAC ticker, while retaining the original report title and former-name provenance. It does not establish that every historical disclosure under Kenya Orchards is an AMAC-period report, so such rows must be labeled as predecessor-name reports rather than renamed annual reports.
