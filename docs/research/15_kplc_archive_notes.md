# Kenya Power first-party archive notes

## Source
- Webpage title: Annual Reports | Kenya Power
- URL: https://www.kplc.co.ke/annual-reports
- Accessed: 2026-08-27

## Archive structure
Kenya Power’s official archive lists annual reports and financial results, including FY2025 integrated/abridged/audited reports, six-month unaudited trading results for December 2024, FY2024 integrated and audited reports, FY2023 audited and annual reports, six-month results for December 2022, FY2022 annual/audited reports, and unaudited year-end 2021 results. The page is paginated across at least five pages.

## Research use
The visible first page is a strong issuer-hosted source for annual and semi-annual records. Exact direct PDF links must be extracted from the page HTML and the remaining pagination pages should be crawled for older reports. Duplicated FY2025/FY2024 records should be retained only when the documents are materially distinct, with duplicate-file hashes used for deduplication.
