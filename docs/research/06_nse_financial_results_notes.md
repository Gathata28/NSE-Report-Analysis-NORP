# NSE financial-results archive notes

## Source
- Webpage title: Financial Results - Nairobi Securities Exchange PLC
- URL: https://www.nse.co.ke/financial-results/
- Accessed: 2026-08-27

## Observed coverage
The official NSE financial-results page provides year filters from 2015 through 2026 and pagination. The visible 2026 results include Equity Group Holdings, Standard Chartered Bank Kenya, Family Bank, Absa Bank Kenya, Liberty Kenya Holdings, Nation Media Group, Crown Paints Kenya, and Sanlam Allianz Holdings. The page labels these as unaudited financial statements, group results, or other disclosures, including six-month and half-year results.

## Research use
This is a central official fallback source for issuer interim and other financial disclosures when the company website is difficult to access. It is not a substitute for the issuer's own IR PDF when a first-party copy exists. The page needs HTML extraction to enumerate all pagination pages and direct file links. It may provide broader coverage of the 2015–2026 period than the NSE annual-report archive.
