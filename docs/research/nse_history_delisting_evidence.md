# Official NSE History Evidence — Historical Status Limitation

**Source:** [NSE Our Story](https://www.nse.co.ke/our-story/)

**Retrieved:** 27 August 2026

The official NSE history page explains that, after political changes in the former East African Community, decisions affected capital movement and ultimately led to the delisting of companies domiciled in Uganda and Tanzania from the Nairobi Stock Exchange. It also states that, after the EAC collapsed in 1975, the Government of Uganda compulsorily nationalized companies that were quoted or subsidiaries of listed companies.

This page provides historical context but not a complete issuer-by-issuer delisting register. It therefore supports the archive’s rule that current-page absence must not be treated as proof of a specific issuer’s delisting, suspension, absorption, or insolvency. A formal status row requires an explicit issuer, NSE, CMA, or other primary status source.
