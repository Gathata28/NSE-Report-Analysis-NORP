# Co-operative Bank first-party archive notes

## Source
- Webpage title: Integrated Reports | Co-operative Bank of Kenya
- URL: https://www.co-opbank.co.ke/investor-relations/integrated-reports/
- Accessed: 2026-08-27

## Archive structure
The Co-operative Bank’s official investor-relations page provides an Integrated Reports archive with year filters from 2014 through 2026. Visible records include the 2018–2025 Co-op Bank Group integrated reports, each with a Download action. The bank also exposes a separate Financial Statements route at https://www.co-opbank.co.ke/investor-relations/financial-statements/ for periodic and other financial statements.

## Research use
The integrated reports are first-party annual-report documents. The separate financial-statements archive should be crawled for half-year and quarterly reports, and exact direct download hrefs should be extracted from the page HTML rather than inferred from the labels.
