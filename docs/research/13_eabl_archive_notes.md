## Archive structure
EABL’s official archive lists full-year and half-year results materials by year from 2008 through 2026. It includes financials, presentations, booklets, press releases, and press advertisements. The visible current records cover full-year and half-year 2026, with historical records extending back to 2008.

## Scope classification
The page is a strong first-party source for annual and semi-annual reporting materials. “Financials” or “financial results” documents should be classified as statement/report records; presentations, booklets, press releases, and press advertisements should be retained as related periodic reporting materials but marked by subtype. The page is subject to a cookie/age overlay in the visual browser, but the public report links and metadata are accessible in the page HTML.
