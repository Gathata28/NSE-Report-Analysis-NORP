# Safaricom first-party archive notes

## Source

## Archive structure
Safaricom’s official page lists annual reports from 2009 through 2026 and provides a “Download PDF” action for each year; several years also have “View Online” versions. The page links to a separate results area that exposes FY and HY report materials, including the HY26 investor presentation, HY26 earnings booklet and press release, plus FY25 results materials.

## Research use
This is an issuer-hosted primary archive with unusually broad historical coverage. The final index should include the annual-report PDF URLs, the separate financial-report/results archive URL, and any quarter-specific documents exposed by that archive. Annual reports must be distinguished from investor booklets and press releases even when they relate to the same period.
