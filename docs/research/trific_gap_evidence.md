# TRIFIC Green USD I-REIT — Periodic-Report Gap Evidence

**Reference date:** 27 August 2026

The official [TRIFIC I-REIT page](https://trific.co.ke/i-reit/) exposes a prospectus and offering/allotment information. It does not expose an annual report, interim financial statements, quarterly report, or other completed reporting-period financial statement in the inspected public content. The prospectus is intentionally excluded from the periodic-report index.

The official [TRIFIC Resources page](https://trific.co.ke/resources/) lists generic resource categories, years, and quarters but did not expose a public report item in the extracted content. The official [TRIFIC News page](https://trific.co.ke/news/) contains newsletters and promotional/insight posts, not issuer financial statements. A bounded scan of TRIFIC’s public WordPress resource endpoint, [https://trific.co.ke/wp-json/wp/v2/resource?per_page=100](https://trific.co.ke/wp-json/wp/v2/resource?per_page=100), returned resource records but no annual, financial-statement, quarterly, interim, or audit report title and no direct PDF attachment link. Therefore TRFC remains the single current-NSE coverage gap; this is not a delisting conclusion.
