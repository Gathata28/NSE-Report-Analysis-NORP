# National Bank of Kenya historical archive

**Official source page:** https://www.nationalbank.co.ke/investor-relations

The official National Bank investor-relations page contains National Bank annual and periodic financial disclosures from 2009 through 2026, including full-year, Q1, H1, and Q3 materials. It also includes integrated and abridged financial statements for 2013–2018. The page separately lists KCB Group documents after KCB acquired National Bank; those KCB documents must not be indexed as National Bank historical reports.

The page’s corporate-history text states that KCB Group acquired 100% of National Bank. This makes the page a useful first-party archive for the historical National Bank issuer, while preserving issuer identity separately from successor-company reports.

Collection date: 2026-08-27. The archive includes some 2026 National Bank-branded financial records even though National Bank is not in the current 66-row NSE universe; status, issuer identity, and period labels should be verified from each linked PDF.
