## Archive structure
Safaricom’s official financial-reporting page provides Full Year and Half Year views. The Full Year view visibly lists FY08–FY26 investor presentations and, for most years, results booklets, press commentaries, or press releases. The page separately links to the annual-report archive and, through the “Half Year” view, to semi-annual materials. The visible current-year records include FY26, FY25, and FY24 results materials.

## Scope classification
The page’s investor presentations, earnings booklets, and press releases are periodic reporting materials but are not automatically equivalent to audited annual reports or unaudited financial statements. The final dataset will classify them separately and retain only documents whose titles/content indicate annual, half-year, or quarter reporting, with `document_subtype` distinguishing statement, report, booklet, presentation, commentary, and press release.
