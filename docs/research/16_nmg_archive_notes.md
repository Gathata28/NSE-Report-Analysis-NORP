## Archive structure
Nation Media Group’s official financial-reports page lists annual or financial reports for 2013–2025, including 2025 integrated report and financial statements, 2024–2021 annual reports and financial statements, 2020 annual report and financial statements, and earlier 2019–2013 financial/annual reports. Each record has a Download action and, for recent years, a Visit Microsite action.

## Periodic reporting route
The official Investor Updates page is the complementary route for current unaudited and half-year results. The financial-reports page itself is primarily annual-report focused and should be indexed separately from Investor Updates.
