# Historical NSE source notes

## Official NSE history page
Source page: https://www.nse.co.ke/our-story/

The official NSE history page states that during 1963–1970 the exchange operated as a regional market with securities from Kenya, Tanzania, and Uganda, and that changing political regimes ultimately led to delisting companies domiciled in Uganda and Tanzania from the Nairobi Stock Exchange. It notes the compulsory nationalization of Ugandan companies after the East African Community collapsed in 1975. The page provides historical narrative but not a complete table of all current, suspended, delisted, absorbed, or formerly listed issuers.

Collection date: 2026-08-27. Use this source as historical context, not as a substitute for an authoritative historical issuer universe. The historical/delisted issuer table remains to be built from NSE/CMA notices, official historical lists, and company-level records.
