# Absa Bank Kenya first-party archive notes

## Source
- Webpage title: Investor relations
- URL: https://www.absabank.co.ke/investor-relations/
- Accessed: 2026-08-27

## Archive structure
Absa’s official page states that documents before 10 February 2020 were published under Barclays Bank of Kenya Limited. The page provides annual-report years from 2025 back to 2004 and a separate financial-results archive with current and historical reporting. Direct PDFs include Absa annual reports and financial statements, legacy Barclays annual reports, and current 2026/2025/2024 financial-result documents.

## Research use
Absa is a high-value historical source because it preserves the predecessor-name archive. The final index should retain issuer identity as `Absa Bank Kenya PLC (formerly Barclays Bank of Kenya Ltd)` for pre-2020 records, distinguish annual reports from financial-results PDFs, and exclude proxy, AGM, policy, governance, and sustainability documents unless separately requested.
