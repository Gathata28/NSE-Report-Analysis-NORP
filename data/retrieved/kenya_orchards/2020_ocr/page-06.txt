Director Orientation

Directors undergo an induction process upon appointment during which they are given a full briefing on the company. This
includes meetings with key executives. tours of operational sites and presentations. Thereafter, in order to assist Directors
to maintain an appropriate level of knowledge of the company and is operations, Directors undertake site visits and are
provided with regular updates and briefings on current and emerging issues.
