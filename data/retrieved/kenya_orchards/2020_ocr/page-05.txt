Kenya Orchards Limited (KOL) and its Board of Directors are committed to achieving the highest standards of corporate

corporate governance practices in respect to 2019 are detailed in this statement, The Board of Directors and entire

Board Composition

Directors are elected by the Shareholders based on the skills and experience they are able to bring to Board
deliberations on current and emerging issues. In addition, the Board seeks 10 ensure that the size of the Board as well
the blend of skills within its membership, is conducive to effect discussion and efficient decision making. In
acvordance with the company’s Memorandum and Articles of Association, one third of the Directors are required to
retire trom office annually. The Directors have developed and adopted a policy on the appointment, tenure and
retirement of Directors.
