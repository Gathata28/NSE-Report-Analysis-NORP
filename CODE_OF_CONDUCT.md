# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors and maintainers are expected to communicate respectfully, discuss evidence and code rather than people, acknowledge uncertainty, and avoid harassment, discrimination, or disclosure of private information.

## Research and data conduct

Use public and properly attributed sources. Preserve source provenance, distinguish facts from inferences, disclose uncertainty, and do not publish private research, credentials, personal data, or material that a contributor is not authorized to redistribute.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or decline contributions that violate this standard. Serious or repeated violations may result in suspension of participation. Report concerns privately using the process in `SECURITY.md`.
